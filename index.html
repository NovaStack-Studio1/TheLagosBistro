Our Story

Lagos Bistro was started on one idea: great Nigerian food and a proper bar under one roof, served warmly. Our kitchen cooks everything from a quick breakfast to a full seafood platter, and our bar pours everything from zobo-style mocktails to Moët. Come as you are and bring everyone.

FAQ

How do I pay?

Add items to your order, then tap "Pay with Paystack". You can pay by card, bank transfer or USSD.

Are taxes included in menu prices?

No. VAT (7.5%), service charge (5%) and entertainment tax (5%) are added to your bill.

Do soups come with swallow?

Yes, all soups come with your choice of swallow. Extra swallow is ₦3,000.

Do you have food for kids?

Yes. Our kids' menu has Mini Sliders, Hog Dogs and Eko Chicken Fries.
